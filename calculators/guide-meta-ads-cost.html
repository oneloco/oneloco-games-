How Much Do Meta Ads Cost for a Trucking Insurance Agency?

There is no fixed price for Facebook and Instagram ads. You set your own budget, and Meta charges you as people see and click your ads. Most small trucking insurance agencies start at about $50 to $100 a day in ad spend (about $1,500 to $3,000 a month), paid straight to Meta, and then judge the ads by how many bound policies they produce, not by the price of a lead.

What you actually pay for

  • Ad spend. Paid to Meta with your own card. You pick the daily budget. You can pause any time.
  • Creative. Videos and images that speak to owner-operators and small fleets. You make these yourself or hire help.
  • The follow-up system. A quote form, fast texts and emails, and a CRM. Ads only bring the lead. Follow-up is what turns it into a policy.
  • Management (if you hire it). Someone to build, test and watch the ads each week. Fees vary by provider and scope.

What makes trucking ads cost more or less

  1. Who you target. New authorities, owner-operators and small fleets all cost differently to reach.
  2. Your states. Some states have more trucks and more agencies competing for the same people.
  3. The offer. A clear "get a quote" form with a USDOT check usually beats a vague "learn more."
  4. The form. More questions mean fewer leads, but often better ones. Fewer questions mean more leads, but more junk.
  5. The creative. Ads that look and sound like a trucker's real problem get cheaper clicks than stock photos.
  6. Time of year. Ad prices rise in Q4 when big retailers flood Meta.
  7. Speed to lead. Slow follow-up doesn't change the ad price, but it raises what you pay per bound policy, because fewer leads bind.

How much budget do you need to start?

A simple plan for a small agency:

  • Weeks 1–2: test. About $50 a day. Two or three ads. One quote form. Learn what truckers respond to.
  • Weeks 3–6: keep what works. Turn off ads that bring no quotes. Raise the winners slowly — about 15–20% at a time, then wait a few days.
  • Month 2 and after: judge by binds. Most trucking quotes don't bind in the first week, so give it time before you decide.

Rule of thumb: only spend what your team can follow up on. If nobody answers new leads fast, more ad spend just buys more leads that go cold.

Cost per lead vs. cost per bound policy

Cost per lead is easy to see in Ads Manager. It is also easy to fool you. A cheap lead that never answers costs more than an expensive lead that binds.

The number that pays the bills is ad spend ÷ bound policies = cost per bound policy. To get it, you need your ads connected to your CRM, so every policy you bind can be traced back to the ad that brought it.

Our real experience

On one commercial trucking insurance broker's account, $55,424 in Meta ads led to 132 bound policies and $2.6M+ in premium written — about $420 in ads per bound policy. The median lead took 30 days to bind.

Commission math (labelled, not a promise): at an assumed 10% commission, that works out to about $2,000 provable and about $2,650 estimated in commission per policy.

Source: ad spend from Meta Ads Manager; policies and premium from the agency's own records. One account. Results vary.

Example math (not a forecast): at about $420 in ads per bound policy, $1,500 a month in ad spend would be about 3–4 bound policies. Your number will be different. It depends on your market, your follow-up speed and your close rate. Track your own lead → quote → bind numbers from day one.

FAQ

How much should a trucking insurance agency spend on Facebook ads per month?

Many small agencies start around $1,500 to $3,000 a month in ad spend ($50–100 a day). Larger agencies with several agents to work leads often spend more. Start small, then raise the budget only when your team can handle more leads.

Is Facebook or Instagram better for trucking insurance leads?

Both run from the same Meta ad account. Test them together and let your bind data tell you which works better for your agency.

How long before Meta ads bring bound policies?

Leads can come in the first days. Binds take longer. On the account above, the median lead took 30 days to bind. Plan to judge results over months, not days.

Do I pay Meta per lead?

Usually no. Most lead campaigns charge as people see your ads. Your cost per lead is what you spent divided by the leads you got.

Can I run trucking insurance ads with a small budget?

Yes. A small budget can test offers and creative. The key is fast follow-up, so the few leads you get don't go cold.
