How it works

Two machines. One goal: more bound policies.

We fill your pipeline and the seats to work it.

Lead Machine

Fills your pipeline with quote requests and works them until they bind.

  1. Quote requests come to you. Meta ads on your own account send truckers to a quote form in your agency's name. The form checks the USDOT.
  2. Every request gets an answer. A text and an email go out. A text bot books the call for your agent.
  3. Every quote gets followed up, until it is bound or closed. Old quotes get a second look.
  4. Renewals get worked. Outreach starts before each renewal date, for clients and prospects.
What is in it
  • Meta ads for quote requests
  • Trucker quote form with USDOT check
  • Speed-to-lead texts and emails
  • Call booking by text
  • Quote follow-up and dead-quote reactivation
  • Renewal campaigns
  • A monthly Ad-to-Bind report

Recruiting Machine

Fills the seats to work those leads.

  1. Hiring ads reach licensed people: P&C and commercial agents, CSRs and sub-agents.
  2. A recorded overview does the first pitch, so the owner does not repeat it on every call.
  3. One text books the interview.
  4. Hand-off to licensing. Past candidates get a second look. Current staff never get recruiting messages.
What is in it
  • Hiring ads
  • Recorded overview video
  • One-text interview booking
  • Licensing hand-off
  • Past-candidate reactivation
  • Roster guard

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The checklist, the playbook and the 20-minute review cost nothing.

Who is it for?

Owners and managers of independent trucking insurance agencies, including solo agents, who write owner-operators and small fleets.

Do I need ads running already?

No. The checklist works on quote requests from anywhere: web forms, calls and referrals. The review covers what you have today.

Are the leads shared or resold?

No. The ads run on your agency's own Meta account, so every quote request goes only to you. The ad account, the leads and the data stay yours, even if you stop working with us.

Where do these numbers come from?

One commercial trucking insurance broker's Meta ads. Ad spend comes from Meta Ads Manager; bound policies and premium come from the agency's own records. We never name clients. Results vary.

Do you sell insurance?

No. Global TLM does not sell insurance. Our clients are trucking insurance agencies. A trucker who asks for a quote deals with the agency.

What does it cost?

The checklist, the playbook and the review call are free. Paid work starts with a Growth Machine Audit. You get a written scope and price before anything is built.

Can you help us hire agents?

Yes. The Recruiting Machine uses hiring ads and follow-up to bring in licensed agents, CSRs and sub-agents.
