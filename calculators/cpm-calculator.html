The terms

CPM
Cost per 1,000 impressions. Ad spend ÷ impressions × 1,000.
Impressions
How many times your ad was shown. One person can see it more than once.
CTR
Click-through rate. Clicks ÷ impressions.
CPC
Cost per click. Ad spend ÷ clicks.
CPL
Cost per lead. Ad spend ÷ quote requests.
Cost per bind
Ad spend ÷ bound policies. The number that matters most.
